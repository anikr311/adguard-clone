How it works

AdLocko runs a local VPN that only carries DNS traffic. Ad and tracker domains are answered locally with an empty address, everything else goes to the DNS resolver you choose in Settings. No other traffic enters the tunnel.

Privacy

AdLocko has no account, no analytics and no server of its own. Counters and the blocked log never leave this phone. The only network calls the app makes are your DNS queries to the resolver above and the optional blocklist update from GitHub.
